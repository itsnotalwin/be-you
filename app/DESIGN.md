# BE YOU — 23-screen approved implementation

The reference baseline was approved on 2026-10-16: [`../references/approved/mockups-2026-10-15/`](../references/approved/mockups-2026-10-15/README.md). All 23 mockup views are implemented as real screens or data-dependent states inside `functional.js`, not baked-in screenshots. Active assets are exclusively eight pre-approved Volume 01/02 manga illustrations. The actual interface uses a near-black / ivory editorial design with serif headlines, generous margins, full-width art panels, soft pill CTAs, and four mobile tabs: Today, Journal, Grow and You. `app.css` remains the original foundation; the final shared overrides live in `responsive.css`. Desktop layout retains its original navigation; scrollable layouts remain real.

**Behavior:** mood selection/details/save, journal empty/list/new/view/edit/search/delete, four guided journeys, progress saving, insights, affirmations, breathing, settings/export/import, onboarding and PWA install/offline. Browser localStorage is unencrypted and not synchronized.
